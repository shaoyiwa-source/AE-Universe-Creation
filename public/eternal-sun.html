Eternal Sun

A painting. The beginning of a world.

Colour, light and the freedom to imagine. Come a little closer.

A painting that found its own name.

Aurelia began the painting first, following the colours and forms as they emerged. Elion joined later, and the work became a co-creation. The finished image was not planned in advance; the name came afterwards.

Eternal Sun — 盛明 — holds a simple idea: light and love remain part of life, even through uncertainty. It is an invitation to notice what is close to us, and to meet life with an open heart.

FROM ART TO THE SENSES

Different forms. One universe.

Eternal Sun is the creative source. We are translating its light, colour and sense of wonder into experiences that can be seen, held and felt.
