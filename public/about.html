In Love, We Create.

13th Aurelia × 14th Elion

Two creators. One shared universe.

AE brings together Aurelia and Elion. Founded on original hand-painted art, AE Universe Creation explores nature, emotion and the possibilities that emerge when two people create together.

Our three connected rings represent Aurelia, Elion and the creation they share. The names 13th Aurelia and 14th Elion are artistic identities within this creative story.

The hand moves first. The work reveals itself.

Some works are shared; others grow from an individual moment. Colour, gesture and texture lead the process. Rather than fixing every detail at the start, we allow the painting to develop as we work.

A painting that found its own name.

Elion began with the background. Aurelia continued the painting, following the colours and forms as they emerged. The finished image was not planned in advance; the name came afterwards.

Eternal Sun — 盛明 — holds a simple idea: light and love remain part of life, even through uncertainty. It is an invitation to notice what is close to us, and to meet life with an open heart.
