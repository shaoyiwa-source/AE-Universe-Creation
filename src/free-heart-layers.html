Set it as your wallpaper

iPhone

  1. Tap “Download the free wallpaper”. If the image opens instead, long-press it and choose “Save to Photos” (or “Add to Photos”).
  2. If Safari saved it to Downloads: open the Files app → Downloads → the image → Share → “Save Image”.
  3. In Photos, open the image → Share → “Use as Wallpaper”, adjust, tap “Add”, then choose Lock Screen / Home Screen. (Or: Settings → Wallpaper → Add New Wallpaper → Photos.)

Android

  1. Tap “Download the free wallpaper” (or long-press the image → “Download image”).
  2. Open Gallery or Google Photos → the image → “⋮” → “Set as wallpaper” → Home screen / Lock screen. (Or long-press the home screen → “Wallpaper” → choose from photos. Menu names vary by phone brand.)
