WHO WE ARE

Two creators,
one shared creative world.

We are Aurelia and Elion, the two creators of AE Universe Creation. AE comes from our names.

We began with original hand-painted art, using colour, line and brushstroke to express what we feel in everyday life. Some works we finish on our own; others are begun by one of us and handed to the other to continue. Different ideas meet in the painting, and the work takes on a shape neither of us expected.

Today, we are also letting our paintings step off the canvas, as wallpapers for everyday use, or as art experiences that bring together light, sound and interaction. We hope you find works you love here, and your own way of spending time with art.
