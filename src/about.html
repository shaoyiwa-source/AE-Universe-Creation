OUR STORY

In Love, We Create.

13th Aurelia × 14th Elion

On a meaningful afternoon, sunlight streamed in through the window. Looking at the beauty in front of us, Aurelia suggested, “Let’s paint together.” We wanted one shared painting to hold what we felt in that moment.

Aurelia picked up the brush first. As Aurelia remembers it, the very first strokes were purple, opening outward like a peacock spreading its tail. Aurelia kept painting wherever the hand wanted to move, until the main structure gradually took shape, and then invited Elion to continue.

Elion followed the lines and structure already there, adding new colours and brushstrokes. While keeping the original style, the painting grew richer. And so this painting carries the creative marks of two people.

When it was finished, the two of us looked at the painting and felt that Eternal Sun was the right name for it. Elion wrote the name on the painting, and finally we each signed it, completing Eternal Sun.

This co-creation was also a moment when we came to understand how each of us creates: first leave your own idea, then let the other person’s idea join in, and see what new shape appears.

Later, we began to wonder how more people could see these paintings. At first, we simply wanted to show the works well. Then we tried adding light and sound, and slowly explored touch, games and interaction.

The form can change, but the starting point is always the painting itself. We hope art is not only seen, but can also find its way into everyday life, so that people want to stay, to feel, and even to take part in creating together.
