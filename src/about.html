OUR STORY

In Love, We Create.

13th Aurelia × 14th Elion

Because we love nature, all things in the world and everything beautiful, we want to paint what we feel. Love is where our creating begins.

Eternal Sun was born from a beautiful moment of connection between Elion and me. In that atmosphere we created together, leaving what we felt in the painting. For me, this painting is a keepsake of that moment, and the eternity I want to hold on to.
